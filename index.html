Books

I am coauthor of books exploring mathematical structures revealed through continuum physics. The topics span functional analysis, tensor analysis and differential geometry, the calculus of variations, continuum field theories, and mathematical inequalities.

I belong to a tradition that approaches physical theories not merely as applications of mathematics but as rich sources of mathematical ideas.

My writing has moved naturally across disciplinary boundaries. Rather than staking out an area, I sought a center and a voice.
